The two hero flags were downloaded on 2026-11-02 at 2400px wide as JPEG from `images.unsplash.com` (`w=2400&q=80&fm=jpg`), and the three older Why join photographs at 1600px wide (`w=1600&q=80&fm=jpg`). Both sets are unedited.

The sixteen photographs added later on 2026-11-02 were downloaded at 2400px wide (Unsplash `w=2400&q=80&fm=jpg`; Pexels `auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&w=2400`, the Baghdad aerial as JPEG from its PNG source), then resized and re-encoded as progressive JPEG to keep each file under 600 KB: 2400px wide for the full-width banners, 1800px for the two cities frame, 1600px for the flagpoles and the Why join row, 1400px for the portrait Iraqi flag and 1200px for the Why Iraq cards. Three were cropped: `agriculture-date-palm.jpg` to a 1200 × 1000 window around the dates, `baghdad-aerial-tigris.jpg` to the lower 64% of the frame (dropping most of the empty sky), and `geometric-lattice.jpg` to a 2400 × 1500 band from the middle of the portrait original. No colour or content was altered.

The places named for the Basra and Tigris photographs come from the photographs' own listings and tags and have not been checked independently. The container port photograph (Timelab, Hong Kong) that the trade growth row used before was replaced on 2026-11-02 and its file removed.
